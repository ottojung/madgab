## Scope fence: what this front must not do

[w-6b2f04](w-6b2f04.md) owns the **global budget across segmentations** and
the enumeration loop that spends it. To keep the two fronts composable
rather than contending:

- Do **not** implement a global emission/pop counter, a per-segmentation
  share, round-robin budget division, structure-aware budget allocation, or
  shared-prefix/trie work across segmentations. Those are `6b2f04`'s
  mechanisms 1, 2 and 3. If your audit concludes one of them is the only
  thing that works, write that in this item as a finding and stop.
- Do **not** change `LEXICAL_BRANCH_KEEP`, `LEXICAL_COMBINATIONS_PER_SEGMENTATION`
  or `LEXICAL_HEAP_POP_LIMIT`; the measurements above price those.
- Do **not** move `axes::*` weights, the scoring functions, or the
  `approximate_output_is_locked` baseline.
- Do **not** touch `approximate_finds_classic_madgab_resegmentation` or relax
  any assertion.

A mechanism that lives in a *different* place — for example the composition
or ordering of the per-span shortlist that feeds each slot, or the
retention/ordering of segmentations entering the enumeration, or a pruning
that removes work rather than reallocating it — is in scope, provided you
first show by measurement that it changes recall for a spread of real
targets and not just for the canonical pair.

## What "a mechanism worth landing" must satisfy

- It must be **general**: it may not know anything about any word,
  substring, clue or word sequence. Prove it with `git grep -i -E` on your
  own branch: nothing in `src/` may match
  `wreck|beach|recognize|justice|stupid|dupe|came|hid` outside unit tests,
  comments and the pre-existing `src/main.rs` doc examples.
- It must still **bound** something, with named arithmetic. Say what the
  previous bound was (memory, time, pool size) and what bounds it after.
- It must be justified on a **spread of real targets**, not only the
  canonical two: report candidate-pool size and wall clock before and after
  for at least three others, including a long target such as
  `congratulations on your promotion`.
- Prefer a small coherent change. A measurement record with no landed change
  is an acceptable and useful outcome for this item; a large rewrite is not.
- Temporary instrumentation, sweep scripts and `zz_*` survey tests must not
  reach `post-milestone-acceptance`.
- Regression tests express external behaviour on a spread of targets, not
  implementation details.
