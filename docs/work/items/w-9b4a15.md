# Read the clue's word count instead of SHAPE: repair the one objectively wrong thing in the head

## Goal

Make the shipped top 50 for a target **acoustically better than the pool mean** rather than worse,
by replacing the `SHAPE` term — the least efficient term in the objective — with a word-count
parsimony axis that nothing else in the objective reads. This is a **general** quality improvement
available at zero cost. It does **not** reach the canonical case-2 tuple and does not claim to; see
*Successor scope* below.

## The evidence, already measured

[../REPORT-3a8c05.md](../REPORT-3a8c05.md), integrated at this item's base, priced the scoring
surface exhaustively and found one candidate that costs nothing and improves the objective's
behaviour. Verbatim from its §5 note 1:

> C1b repairs the one objectively wrong thing in the head. The shipped top 50 is acoustically
> *worse* than the pool mean (lift **−0.0180**); under C1b the same target's top 50 is acoustically
> **better** than the pool mean (lift **+0.0570**), and its `PUNCH` lift goes from +0.1526 to
> −0.0274 and its `RHYTHM` head stops being a monotone artefact of "six short words".

The candidate is **C1b**: `SHAPE` weight 0.05 → 0, `NOVELTY` 0.15 → `PARSIMONY` 0.15, where

```text
PARSIMONY = 1 - |w_clue - w_target| / max(w_clue, w_target)
```

That report also established:

* nothing in the shipped objective reads the clue's **word count at all**;
* the term being displaced, `SHAPE` at weight 0.05, contributes only **+0.0209** of head lift —
  the least efficient term in the score;
* **the green case is provably immune**: `recognize speech`'s pool top 200 is *100%* four-word
  clues (histogram `{4: 200}`), and its `PARSIMONY` head lift is +0.0320 before and after;
* C1b's canonical-case-2 rank is 7,393 — still not in the top 50, and §3's monotone lower bound of
  1,127 makes that unattainable by any monotone objective. This front does not chase it.

The report explicitly declined to ship this itself: "a change that does not advance a stated goal
should be its own item with its own evidence, not smuggled in on the back of this one." This is
that item.
