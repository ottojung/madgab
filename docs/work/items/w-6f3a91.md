# The adjacency walk charges its seed pool against its own pop budget, so on a
# deep-seeded segmentation it can admit nothing at all

## Goal

Two defects in the **mechanism** of the adjacency operator in
[src/adjacency.rs](https://github.com/ottojung/madgab) on `madgab-adjacency`,
found by the read-only review front [w-b4e8d1](w-b4e8d1.md) and filed
separately so that they do not die with a review item. Neither is a style
point: the first can make the whole operator a no-op on the segmentations it
matters most for, and the second puts a per-candidate allocation back into a
family that was closed on measurement.

## Defect 1 (headline): `pops` is spent on the seeds, not on new wordings

`admit` seeds the frontier with **every** root and then runs exactly
`plan.pops` pop iterations
([src/adjacency.rs:151-162](https://github.com/ottojung/madgab), seed loop;
`src/adjacency.rs:165`, `for _ in 0..plan.pops`). A root is already in
`held`, so a pop of a root admits nothing (`src/adjacency.rs:176` is false);
the pop is spent on re-expanding a wording the pool already has. So

```text
admissions <= pops - |pooled|
```

and the walk reaches nothing new whenever `|pooled| >= pops`.

The seeds are not small. `pooled` is every wording this segmentation emitted
(`src/lib.rs:1411` for the profile reserve, `src/lib.rs:1566` for the
traversal), so `|pooled|` is up to the per-segmentation allowance, which is

```text
emit_allowance = depth_ceiling.saturating_sub(held).clamp(1, 64)
depth_ceiling  = structure_depth_ceiling(LEXICAL_GLOBAL_EMISSION_BUDGET, structures)
```

(`src/lib.rs:1256-1259`). `structure_wording_allowance` is
`share_cap(top_n, STRUCTURE_FLOOR)`, and [w-6b2f04](w-6b2f04.md) measures it
at **17** on a real pool of 137-180 structures, widening towards 64 only when
the pool is small. `profile_emitted` adds up to `EMIT_PROFILE_RESERVE` = 16
on top (`src/lib.rs:1417-1418` leaves the traversal `emit_allowance -
profile_emitted`, and `pooled` holds both). So `|pooled|` runs roughly
`17 + 16 = 33` on a real multi-structure pool.

`ADJACENCY_POPS` is **24** (`src/lib.rs:174`). That is below the seed count it
is documented to have to exceed, so on a real pool the walk spends all 24 pops
popping and re-expanding roots and admits **zero** new wordings for the
majority of segmentations.

The comment directly above the constant says so, and the value contradicts it
(`src/lib.rs:169-173`):

> `It has to exceed the pool the traversal hands it — the pool's own wordings
> are the seeds and are expanded before anything new is reached — so it is set
> above the traversal's own share of the allowance rather than at it.`

24 is not above the traversal's share (17) plus the profile reserve (16); it is
below both. The same comment was correct when the value was 96 and commit
`9767caf` lowered it to 24 without updating it. **This is a live WIP
contradiction between a named invariant and the constant that is supposed to
satisfy it, in the same file, with no test covering the seed-heavy case.**

The `every_admission_is_one_substitution_from_a_held_wording` test added in
`9767caf` uses 3 roots against `pops: 48`, so it cannot see this: the
seed-heavy regime is exactly the untested one.

### What would settle it

Either (a) charge admissions, not pops, and let the seed expansion be free or
separately bounded — e.g. bound the number of *new* wordings admitted and bound
the seed expansion separately, so the two numbers cannot starve each other; or
(b) derive `pops` from an observable property of the input, as
[w-c1d3a7](w-c1d3a7.md) criterion 4 requires, e.g.
`pops = pooled.len() + ADJACENCY_RESERVE`, so the invariant at `src/lib.rs:170`
holds by construction instead of by hand. Either way the arithmetic must be
named and the stale comment must go.

## Defect 2: `best_children` allocates per *probed* candidate, not per kept
one, and its cost is independent of `per_slot`

`best_children` builds a full owned `Node` — including
`tuple: scratch.clone()` — for **every** `i in 0..width` before the retention
gate (`src/adjacency.rs:99-116`). With `keep = ADJACENCY_PER_SLOT` = 2, at most
2 of those clones per slot per pop are ever retained; the rest are dropped.

The cost per pop is therefore `depth × width` probes and
`depth × width` allocations, with `width` bounded by `SPAN_SHORTLIST` = 160
(`src/lib.rs:123`, applied to the edge match list at `src/lib.rs:819`) and
`depth` = the segmentation's span count. Per segmentation that is
`24 × depth × 160` clones; at `depth = 12` that is ~46k allocations, and the
operator runs once per retained segmentation (up to `SEGMENTATION_KEEP` = 256).

Two fences are involved:

- **per-candidate allocations (w-d17a62)** is a closed family, "not to be
  reopened" per [w-c1d3a7](w-c1d3a7.md). This reintroduces one allocation per
  candidate probe on the hottest new path in the search.
- **"bounded with named arithmetic"** (w-c1d3a7 criterion 4). The module doc
  at `src/adjacency.rs:30-37` claims the two named bounds "keep it from
  turning into a second product enumeration". That is true of the *frontier
  size* and false of the *work*: lowering `per_slot` from 4 to 2, which is what
  `9767caf` did, does not reduce the probe count at all, because the probe loop
  is bounded by `width` and not by `keep`.

The clone is also unnecessary: `scratch` is already a scratch buffer, so the
retained child can take `scratch.clone()` only when the child is actually
pushed, and the comparison against the eviction candidate needs only `key`.

## Completion criteria

1. The seed/pop accounting is settled one way or the other, with the arithmetic
   named, and the comment at `src/lib.rs:169-173` agrees with the value.
2. A regression test covers the seed-heavy regime: `|roots| >= pops`, and the
   walk is shown either to admit nothing (with that stated as the accepted
   behaviour) or to admit the intended number of new wordings. The existing
   `every_admission_is_one_substitution_from_a_held_wording` test does not
   cover it.
3. `best_children` allocates per *retained* child, not per probed child, and
   the module's "two named bounds" claim at `src/adjacency.rs:30-37` is either
   made true of the work as well as the frontier, or restated to say what is
   actually bounded.
4. If w-d17a62 is genuinely being reopened, say so in this item with the
   before/after allocation counts, per
   [w-b4e8d1](w-b4e8d1.md)'s standard for justifying a re-baseline.
5. `cargo test --release --lib`, `--test corpus_integration`,
   `--test exact_determinism` and `--test approx_determinism` reported as
   measured, with the known single blocker
   `approximate_finds_classic_madgab_resegmentation` reported verbatim rather
   than assumed.
6. Wall clock and pool size on both canonical targets and at least two
   non-canonical targets, before and after, since both defects are about cost.
