   proposal at `--top 50`. So it is dropped **before the pool is formed**: no

2. **The reviewed reserve-shape fix that raises the deep cap is still not
   integrated, and the constant on the current head still forbids the shape.**
   `src/lib.rs:212` on `post-milestone-acceptance` is
   `const EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP: usize = 3;` while `coverage_tuples`
   (`src/lib.rs:384`) walks slot subsets breadth-before-depth and emits
   subsets of size `1..=max_deep`. The change that raised the cap to 4 and made

   `sweep_index`, `src/lib.rs:302`); the **depth-cap** half was not.

So the concrete question this front owns is narrow and answerable: **on the
current head, is `EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP = 3` the binding reason the requested
wording is never offered as a tuple, and is raising it justified by its own
arithmetic rather than by the phrase?** The independent arm
[w-5d2a91](w-5d2a91.md) re-derives the depth measurement so the implementer is
not the only source of it.

   four deep, on a three-axis-old tree). If it is four, the reserve on this head

   members 47 -> 47, because the *order* was the gate then. Re-measure that

   than assume it.

A refutation of hypothesis 1 is an acceptable and useful result; record it and
route on the next gate.
