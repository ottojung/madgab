### What landed on `784deaa`, and why it is general

Two changes, both in the lexical phase's affordability model, both derived
from budgets the search already respects. Neither mentions any word,
clue or substring of either acceptance example.

1. **Per-slot affordability, read one candidate at a time**
   (`slot_is_affordable`, `src/lib.rs`). A candidate is pushed only if
   `committed(prefix) + min(later slots) + cost(candidate) <= total_budget`
   — exactly the tail of the additive sum `build` already applied at the
   leaf, decided where it can still save work. Nothing `build` would have
   kept is rejected. Measured discarded fraction at the shipped budgets:
   **669 of 15 875** built wordings on one target, **87 of 13 640** on
   another, so it is nearly inert by default and binds when a caller
   tightens `total_budget`. Stated in the code as a bound, not claimed as
   a win.

2. **The traversal's opening width, derived rather than uniform**
   (`affordable_opening_width`, `src/lib.rs`). A best-first walk over `d`
   slots reaches its first wording only after `1 + w + ... + w^(d-1)` pops.
   At `w = 10` and a 4 000-pop limit: `d = 4` needs 1 111 and reaches its
   first wording; `d = 5` needs 11 111 and does not. So a segmentation
   past the crossover was not *poorly* funded, it was funded with a budget
   that cannot be spent at all. Measured on the default release path,
   **27 of 256** retained segmentations emitted no wording whatsoever on
   `It's just a stupid game`, **109** on `a whole lot of trouble`, **84**
   on `the cat sat on the mat`, each after spending its full 4 000 pops
   out of the *global* pop budget, paid for by the segmentations that do
   emit. The new function takes the largest `w` whose series fits the pop
   limit, capped by `LEXICAL_BRANCH_STAGE_0`, so below the crossover
   nothing changes and `next_branch_stage`'s widening ladder is untouched.

**Purpose of each bound, before and after.**

| bound | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| per-slot candidate width | whole contribution list; cost budget checked only at the leaf | candidates the total budget cannot pay for are never pushed; the index space is the affordable one |
| traversal opening width | `LEXICAL_BRANCH_STAGE_0`, uniform in the slot count, i.e. a function of nothing | largest `w` with `1 + w + ... + w^(d-1) <= LEXICAL_HEAP_POP_LIMIT`, capped by `LEXICAL_BRANCH_STAGE_0` and by the widest list |
| `LEXICAL_HEAP_POP_LIMIT` | 4 000 per segmentation; unpayable past ~4 slots | unchanged; now actually payable at every slot count, because the width is derived from it |
| `EMIT_PROFILE_RESERVE` / `coverage_tuples` / `sweep_index` / `EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP` | 16 / breadth-before-depth / floor `LEXICAL_BRANCH_STAGE_0` / 3 | **unchanged — not this front's territory** (9c4d21 owns the reserve) |
