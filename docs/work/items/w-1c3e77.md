## Why this is a new item

1. **[w-d4a90b](w-d4a90b.md) localised the loss to enumeration, not the

   `1.11951981` <= `1.5`, exactly one complete lattice alignment) and scoreable
   at `0.79990129077367222`, i.e. `0.09785131568084526` below the worst visible
   proposal at `--top 50`. So it is dropped **before the pool is formed**: no
   score, admission, share-cap or cutoff change can surface it.

2. **The reviewed reserve-shape fix that raises the deep cap is still not
   integrated, and the constant on the current head still forbids the shape.**
   `src/lib.rs:212` on `post-milestone-acceptance` is
   `const EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP: usize = 3;` while `coverage_tuples`
   (`src/lib.rs:384`) walks slot subsets breadth-before-depth and emits
   subsets of size `1..=max_deep`. The change that raised the cap to 4 and made
   each member of a shape class draw its own coordinate exists, was reviewed
   for generality, and was deliberately **held** pending a breadth measurement
   (commits `a58a49b` and the continuity clause `3b14482` on
   `madgab-pairing-2f7a10`, report `REPORT-2f7a11.md`). The **sweep** half of
   that work *was* integrated (`e123dbb`, per-member coprime sweep rates at
   `sweep_index`, `src/lib.rs:302`); the **depth-cap** half was not.

So the concrete question this front owns is narrow and answerable: **on the
current head, is `EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP = 3` the binding reason the requested
wording is never offered as a tuple, and is raising it justified by its own
arithmetic rather than by the phrase?** The independent arm
[w-5d2a91](w-5d2a91.md) re-derives the depth measurement so the implementer is
not the only source of it.

## Hypotheses, in order

1. **Depth cap.** Count how many of the requested wording's coordinates are
   simultaneously off the traversal's best index in its own slot's ranked list
   on **this** head (the older figures were 7 / 0 / 13 / 99 / 11 per slot, i.e.
   four deep, on a three-axis-old tree). If it is four, the reserve on this head
   cannot express the tuple at any phase, and the 17:47Z pass already measured
   that raising the cap 3 -> 5 with nothing else changed moved canonical
   members 47 -> 47, because the *order* was the gate then. Re-measure that
   arithmetic: the order has since changed (`profile_tuple`, interleaved
   depths, `COVERAGE_SLOT_ROTATION`), so the old refutation may not hold.
2. **Per-structure retention.** The structure `[3, 10, 13, 15, 19]` is
   populated; check whether `SEGMENTATION_KEEP` or the pool's per-structure cap
   is what bounds it, and whether an under-represented structure is crowded
   out by structures with more members.
3. **The cost bound.** `build` compares each tuple's total substitution cost
   against `total_budget` additively. The requested tuple costs
   `1.11951981` of `1.5`, so this is expected to be refuted — measure it rather
   than assume it.

A refutation of hypothesis 1 is an acceptable and useful result; record it and
route on the next gate.
