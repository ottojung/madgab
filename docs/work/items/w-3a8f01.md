# Structure-aware head selection: get a pool-rank-8 clue into the shipped default head

## Goal

Make the **shipped default `--top 10` display** of `madgab --approximate` include a
clue the pool already ranks highly, when the exclusion is caused by the *selection /
diversity* layer collapsing the visible head onto a single resegmentation. The reference
observation is that `wreck a nice beach` is at **pool rank 8** for `recognize speech` and
still is not in the default printed head, because `select_diverse` spends all ten slots on
one resegmentation family.

This must be a **general** rule. No phrase, target, clue, word count, or structure literal
may appear in `src/`.

## Why this front and not another reweighting

`docs/work/REPORT-5e9c41.md` §Recommendation is the source and it is explicit:

> a scoring axis that improves every clue's local quality **concentrates the pool head in
> one resegmentation**, and `share_cap` is then decided by a structure, not by a clue.
> That is a selection-policy question and it is explicitly out of this front's scope.

That is now the highest-value untried shape. It is not a re-specification of any row in
`docs/work/OBSTRUCTION-MAP.md` §2:

* row 10 (objective / ranking) is **closed** and its refutation is weight-free — a monotone
  reweighting cannot move the canonical. This front is **not** a reweighting.
* `docs/work/REPORT-a4d10c.md` measured the selection and diversity layer as a **pass-through
  at the shipped default** for the *pre-`WORST_WORD`* head. `WORST_WORD` at `0.05` changed
  that: distinct structures per list fell **6.71 → 5.79**, dominant-structure share rose
  **0.3292 → 0.4000**, and the printed head became **10 of 10 one ending**. The pass-through
  measurement no longer holds and was never re-run on this head.
