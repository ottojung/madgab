# Price and, if it survives, implement the adjacent-slot (boundary-move-as-one-event) substitution charge

## Goal

The substitution cost charges a resegmentation that moves a word boundary
**per slot**, so a reading that splits one target word across two clue words
is charged twice. Give the scorer a general way to see such a pair of
adjacent slots as **one** event — either by scoring the concatenated clue IPA
against the target IPA, or by admitting a cost for an adjacent slot pair
bounded by that pair's own phonetic distance — and measure whether the
canonical case-2 clue moves into the visible band as a consequence.

This is a **score** front, not another reach front. Reach is closed by
measurement (see below); this is the one live surface that can change what the
already-enumerated lattice is *worth*.
