# The printed-set gate: a pool-resident candidate the selector refuses to print

## Goal

Determine, by measurement and then by a general fix if one exists, why a candidate that sits
**inside the approximate pool at a good rank** does not survive into the **printed top-N proposal
set**, and whether that gate can be moved in a general, phrase-free way that keeps the diversity
and determinism fences green.

This is the layer between *reaching* a clue and *proposing* it. The itinerary's milestone is
stated at the **proposal set** boundary, so a pool-resident-but-unprinted clue fails it exactly as
a pruned clue does.

## Context

`agent-e086cc`'s in-harness sweep measured the fence **F7** (green case present in the *printed*
set) as **red at every admissible objective vector it has run so far**, while **F8** (green case
present in the *pool*) is green everywhere:

```text
A1 (C1d)  pool_rank=48  in_printed=false
A2        pool_rank=48  in_printed=false
A3        pool_rank=200 in_printed=false
A4        pool_rank=662 in_printed=false
F8_green_pool = true at every vector
```

So the case-1 canonical is repeatedly *found* and repeatedly *withheld by the selector*. The
selector side is `select_diverse` and `dedup`; the diversity penalty was previously rescaled by
the score spread *inside the requested top-N* (a few thousandths), and `dedup` treats `this` and
`this'` as different clues.

This front is deliberately **orthogonal to the two live fronts** and must not contend with either:

- [w-e086cc](w-e086cc.md) (`agent-e086cc`, `/workspace/madgab-e086cc`) varies the **objective
  weight vector** and classifies fences F0–F4 against the walk's heap key. F7 is a byproduct there.
- [w-3f6a21](w-3f6a21.md) (`agent-3f6a21`, `/workspace/madgab-pairscore-3f6a21`) works the
  **score function** (adjacent-slot substitution charge).

This front holds the **shipped objective fixed** and studies only the post-pool stage: ordering,
`dedup`, `select_diverse`, the top-N cut, and whatever lock the output path applies.

## Completion criteria

1. A located, cited account (file:line) of the post-pool pipeline from pool to printed proposals,
   and the exact stage that drops a pool-rank-48 candidate, measured rather than inferred.
2. A priced statement of how much printed top-50 lift is available from the selection layer alone
   at the **shipped** objective, with the green case's printed rank before and after.
3. Either a general, phrase-free change with regression tests that keeps
   `no_phrase_hard_coding` 9/9, `emit_coverage` 4/4, `approximate_output_is_locked` and both
   determinism suites green, **or** a priced negative saying the gate is not movable here and
   naming the successor coordinate.
4. No sentence, clue, word, or exact rank from the canonical examples special-cased in code.
5. Push the report and any accepted change to `madgab-printgate-7c9d21`. Never self-merge into
   `post-milestone-acceptance`; never touch `main`.
