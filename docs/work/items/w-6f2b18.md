# Derive segmentation retention from the traversal's own emission census
# instead of the fixed `SEGMENTATION_KEEP = 256`

## Goal

Make approximate mode enumerate `Hits Justice Dupe Hid Came` for
`It's just a stupid game` through general pool-retention behaviour, never a
phrase-specific exception.

This is a **third, independent front**, opened in parallel with
[w-9c4d21](w-9c4d21.md) (coverage reserve) and
[w-1c3e77](w-1c3e77.md) (per-slot affordability) because the adversarial
review [w-a7e2b3](w-a7e2b3.md) found those two to be mutually exclusive in
outcome — one of them must lose — while the retention lever is a *different*
part of the search and does not contend with either one's hunks.

## Context: why retention, and why it is still open

* [w-d4a90b](w-d4a90b.md): the requested wording is absent from the entire
  pool (17 827 candidates at `--top 50`), so it has no rank. It is admissible
  (per-word costs `0.2 / 0.0 / 0.15 / 0.3695 / 0.4`, total `1.11951981`
  <= `1.5`), scoreable at `0.79990129`, and its per-slot ranks are
  `7 / 0 / 13 / 99 / 11`. Exactly one coordinate (slot 3, rank 99) is out
  of reach.
* [w-5f1c04](w-5f1c04.md), [w-be6d21](w-be6d21.md): the global emission
  budget is unreachable by construction — `emit_allowance` is clamped to
  `LEXICAL_COMBINATIONS_PER_SEGMENTATION = 64` per segmentation. Do not
  re-open the budget family.
* [w-a7e2b3](w-a7e2b3.md): front B's affordability model is *arithmetically
  incapable* of the goal (its cap is `<= 7` against a required index of 99),
  and front B has additionally measured that **27 of 109 (and 84 of 256)
  retained segmentations emit nothing at all** after spending 4 000 pops.
  Retention is therefore not merely a candidate lever: a measurable share of
  the retained pool is budget spent on nothing, which is a defect of the
  retention rule itself.
* Every other family is refuted by name: visit order, per-slot truncation,
  adjacency neighbourhood ([w-6f3a91](w-6f3a91.md),
  [w-c1d3a7](w-c1d3a7.md)), cost model, score monotonicity.

The general mechanism this item owns: the number of segmentations the
structural DP retains should be a function of what the traversal actually
does with them — a segmentation that emits nothing is not worth its
retention slot, and the number kept should follow the measured productive
population rather than a hand-picked 256. Every input is a property of the
search's own state, so the change helps other inputs for the same reason.

## Hypotheses, in order

1. **Retention keyed on measured productivity.** Retain segmentations in
   order of what they actually emitted, so a segmentation that produced
   nothing loses its slot to one that produced wordings.
2. **Retention scaled by the productive population.** If the kept count is
   derived from the measured fraction of productive segmentations (and the
   per-segmentation emission ceiling that censors them), the clamp to 64 and
   the 256 ceiling stop being independent constants.
3. **A refutation** of 1 and 2 with the census and arithmetic on record is an
   acceptable and useful result — record it and route on the next gate.
