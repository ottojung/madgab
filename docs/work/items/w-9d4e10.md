# Build the specified per-phone `SIMILARITY` design (D6c) as a general scoring improvement

## Why this item exists

[w-5c11a2](w-5c11a2.md) closed with a **priced negative on the canonical
case-2 alignment plus a complete, implementation-ready specification** for a
different, general change. That specification is now unbuilt work. This item is
the implementation front. It is deliberately *not* a resurrection of w-5c11a2:
that item's measurement is final, its arithmetic is on the current tree, and no
agent should re-derive it.

## The specification, as measured and priced by `c0ff01`

Build the recommended **D6c** design from
[w-5c11a2](w-5c11a2.md)'s *Implementation-ready specification* section:

- normalise the `SIMILARITY` axis **per phone** rather than per word
  (`src/lib.rs:2617-2620` on the tree that was measured), introducing
  `SIMILARITY_COST_PER_PHONE = 0.30` at `src/lib.rs:2786`;
- the swept safe band, its effect on pool size (unchanged by construction) and
  its effect on the relative cutoffs are all recorded in that item. Use those
  numbers. Do **not** re-sweep to find a better-looking setting.

Predicted effects from the specification, to be confirmed or refuted by
measurement on the integrated tree:

- canonical case 1 (`wreck a nice beach` for `recognize speech`) stays in the
  printed set, around printed rank 27;
- the case-2 single-length monopoly breaks: the printed 50 goes from 50/50
  six-word to 5 five-word + 45 six-word;
- the case-2 cutoff moves about -0.002007 and the case-1 cutoff about +0.001771;
- the case-2 alignment is still about -0.094351 out. **This design is not
  expected to reach the canonical case-2 alignment**, and the item is not
  failed if it does not. Its value is the general property below.

## What this change is actually buying

The general property: **`SIMILARITY` currently charges per word, so a clue is
penalised for having more words regardless of how much phonetic material those
words contain.** Two clues over the same target whose words carry equal
per-phone cost but different span lengths therefore score differently on this
axis for a reason that has nothing to do with quality. D6c removes that
artefact. That is a general scoring correctness fix, not a tuning move, and it
is the reason this front is worth running even though it does not reach
canonical case 2.

## Tests required, as specified by w-5c11a2

1. A unit test in `#[cfg(test)]`, in general terms with **no example phrase**:
   two clues over the same target whose words have equal per-phone cost but
   different span lengths score the same on `SIMILARITY`, and a clue whose
   words are individually worse per phone scores strictly below both. Red
   before the change, green after.
2. A regression test at the `generate_pool` or executable boundary: *for a
   short multi-syllable target, the printed proposal set is not a single
   word-count class.* Per w-5c11a2 this is green on neither tree today (both
   canonical targets are 50/50), so write it against a target where the
   property is the one being bought. Red before D6c, green after.
3. `approximate_output_is_locked` **must** be re-locked against the new
   behaviour. In the same commit, fix the w-4e2b19 self-contradicting comment
   in `tests/corpus_integration.rs` (see w-5c11a2's Handoff).

  comments. `tests/no_phrase_hard_coding.rs` strips comments and will not catch

  [w-d3f7a1](w-d3f7a1.md) no further derivation of those refutations may be

  stays red.
