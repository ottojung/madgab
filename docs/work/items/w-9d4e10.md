## The specification, as measured and priced by `c0ff01`

Build the recommended **D6c** design from
[w-5c11a2](w-5c11a2.md)'s *Implementation-ready specification* section:

- normalise the `SIMILARITY` axis **per phone** rather than per word
  (`src/lib.rs:2617-2620` on the tree that was measured), introducing
  `SIMILARITY_COST_PER_PHONE = 0.30` at `src/lib.rs:2786`;
- the swept safe band, its effect on pool size (unchanged by construction) and
  its effect on the relative cutoffs are all recorded in that item. Use those
  numbers. Do **not** re-sweep to find a better-looking setting.

Predicted effects from the specification, to be confirmed or refuted by
measurement on the integrated tree:

- canonical case 1 (`wreck a nice beach` for `recognize speech`) stays in the
  printed set, around printed rank 27;
- the case-2 single-length monopoly breaks: the printed 50 goes from 50/50
  six-word to 5 five-word + 45 six-word;
- the case-2 cutoff moves about -0.002007 and the case-1 cutoff about +0.001771;
- the case-2 alignment is still about -0.094351 out. **This design is not
  expected to reach the canonical case-2 alignment**, and the item is not
  failed if it does not. Its value is the general property below.

## What this change is actually buying

The general property: **`SIMILARITY` currently charges per word, so a clue is
penalised for having more words regardless of how much phonetic material those
words contain.** Two clues over the same target whose words carry equal
per-phone cost but different span lengths therefore score differently on this
axis for a reason that has nothing to do with quality. D6c removes that
artefact. That is a general scoring correctness fix, not a tuning move, and it
is the reason this front is worth running even though it does not reach
canonical case 2.

### What was changed, and nothing else

The change is [w-5c11a2](w-5c11a2.md)'s *Implementation-ready specification*
(D6c), applied literally, with **no re-sweep** and **no re-tuning**:

- `metrics` in `src/lib.rs` now reads
  `cost_per_phone = self.sub_cost_total / total_len.max(1)` and
  `similarity = (1.0 - cost_per_phone / axes::SIMILARITY_COST_PER_PHONE)`.
  `total_len` was already a parameter and is the target's phone count, so
  no signature changed.
- `axes::SIMILARITY_COST_PER_WORD = 1.0` is replaced by
  `axes::SIMILARITY_COST_PER_PHONE = 0.30`, **the value the specification's
  swept safe band [0.15, 0.35] recommended, not re-swept**.
- The axis's doc comment and the constant's doc comment were rewritten to
  describe the per-phone reading and to keep the standing note that
  `SIMILARITY_PER_WORD` (the three enumeration-side proxies) is deliberately
  left on its old scale, with the reason.
- `Metrics` gained a `similarity: f64` field.  It is the only structural
  addition, and it is what lets test 1 assert the axis on its own instead
  of inferring it from `combined` through six other word-count-dependent
  terms.  No score is affected.
- `incremental_aggregates_match_a_full_refold`'s local `refold` helper was
  moved to the per-phone formula, because it is a re-derivation of
  `metrics` and is worthless if it re-derives a different axis.
- **The superseded test was removed, not relaxed.**
  `tests::similarity_is_scored_per_word_not_per_candidate` asserted that the
  axis is *flat in word count at equal per-word cost*, which is precisely
  the specification D6c replaces: under a per-phone reading, `n` words at
  per-word cost `c` cost `n*c` in total and must be charged for all of it.
  It was observed **red** after the change, at
  `3 words at 0.05 per word scored 0.5980468750000001 against
  0.5986979166666667 for 2 words at the same per-word cost`.
  Its replacement is `similarity_is_charged_per_phone_not_per_word`, which
  states the corrected property in the same file, in the same general style,
  and is strictly stronger on the point that matters: it asserts that the
  axis is flat in word count at equal **total** cost, and that it still
  ranks.  It was not weakened, renamed-in-place or `#[ignore]`d; the old
  claim is gone because the specification it encodes is gone.

Nothing else.  No `PUNCH` or `RHYTHM` change, no weight change, no budget
widening, no emission-site touch, no `zz*` file, no `eprintln!` probe, no
env-var knob, no phase timing.
