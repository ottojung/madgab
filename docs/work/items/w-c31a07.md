# Default-visibility ordering: why the canonical case-1 answer sits at display 27 and the shipped default shows 10

## Goal

Improve the *ordering and selection* of completed approximate proposals so that a
high-quality alignment which is already in the pool is surfaced in the visible head, without
any phrase-specific rule.

This is the first unpriced case-2/case-1-adjacent surface left after
[w-2b6a19](w-2b6a19.md) closed the lexicon surface and [w-3c5b18](w-3c5b18.md) closed the
`prune_partials` threshold.

## Why this surface is real and not a repeat

Every named *search* coordinate has now been priced: objective weights and admissible vectors
(`w-e086cc`, empty), the coverage reserve (`w-1a4e8d`), adjacent-slot charging (`w-3f6a21`),
span-shortlist retention (`w-5c1a3e`), the `prune_partials` discard threshold (`w-3c5b18`),
and lexicon/rarity coverage (`w-2b6a19`). All of those are about *which partials exist*.

This item is about what happens to alignments that **do** exist, at the boundary the itinerary's
completion checks are written against. [w-8f0b3d](w-8f0b3d.md) measured the shipped release
binary and found the milestone to be **half false**:

```text
recognize speech:         "wreck a nice beach" at display rank 27 of 50 (score 0.920)
                          for --top 50..1000, and ABSENT at the shipped default --top 10
                          and at --top 25
It's just a stupid game:  "hits justice dupe hid came" absent at every --top 1..1000
```

So the canonical case-1 answer is **present in the pool and reachable at the executable
boundary, and the shipped default simply does not show it.** That is a user-visible defect in
its own right and a general ordering/selection question, not a reach question.

## Completion criteria

1. A measurement, at the executable boundary with the shipped release binary, of **why** the
   canonical case-1 alignment ranks 27th: which stage places it there — the selection/diversity
   layer, the final scorer, or the ordering key — with the stage and rank, not a summary
   adjective.
2. A general change to ordering or selection that lifts a demonstrably high-scoring alignment
   into the visible head, expressed as a property that helps other inputs. If a general change
   cannot be found, a **priced negative** stating what was tried and what it cost is a complete
   result; a negative is not a failure here.
3. A regression test at the library or executable boundary pinning the property. The phrase
   literal must live in `tests/` or a `#[cfg(test)]` module, and `no_phrase_hard_coding` must
   stay **9/0** with `src/` at zero allowlist entries.
4. Case 1 stays **present and visible** at the shipped default `--top 10` after any change, and
   case 2 is not made worse. Re-measure both on the shipped binary; do not assume.
5. Any priced change reports wall clock, pool size and expansions. `cargo fmt` and
   `cargo clippy` if they can run; if not, say so rather than claiming them.

## Constraints

- No phrase-specific hard-coding, and no exception keyed on either canonical sentence or on any
  of the individual clue words. The improvement must be a property of ordering, selection or
  scoring that generalizes.
- Do not change the objective weight vector or the score function's axis set — that lever is
  priced closed by `w-e086cc` (the admissible-vector set is empty), so re-deriving it is waste.
- Do not relax or re-pin `approximate_finds_classic_madgab_resegmentation`, and do not change what
  the CLI prints without recording the change here.
- Work on a focused branch in its own worktree created from `post-milestone-acceptance`. Never
  merge into `main`; integrate into `post-milestone-acceptance`.
