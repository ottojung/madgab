# Stop truncating each slot's candidate list before the traversal sees it

## Goal

The per-segmentation lexical enumeration cannot reach a word that sits deep
in its span's candidate list, because every slot's list is truncated to a
uniform `LEXICAL_BRANCH_KEEP` **before** the product traversal starts. Make
the depth of a slot's list a property of the traversal's budget rather than a
pre-filter applied identically to every slot, so that a word which is deep in
one slot is reachable by the traversal that has budget to spend there.

## Context: what is already established, do not re-measure it

- Every one of the five canonical words survives the per-span shortlist
  (`SPAN_SHORTLIST = 160`). Per-slot ranks inside the span's match list are
  **6-8 / 0 / 10-18 / 99 / 11**; the "hid rank 85" and "hid rank 99" figures
  in this repository's documents are the same word in two different
  orderings, not a disagreement ([w-3e5c7](w-3e5c7.md), integrated as
  `24f3cf7`).
- The canonical segmentation is retained by the structural DP at rank 151 of
  256 and the matcher is innocent ([w-7b41d2](w-7b41d2.md)).
- `LEXICAL_BRANCH_KEEP = 10` truncates each slot's list to ten alternatives
  before enumeration, so `hid`, at walk rank 99, is **never expanded**. No
  visit order can reach a node that is never pushed.
- Shortlist reordering, walk-rank ordering, and a budget-derived *per-slot*
  cap have all been implemented and **refuted by measurement**
  ([w-7b41d2](w-7b41d2.md), [w-04f83f](w-04f83f.md)). The per-slot cap failed
  because per-word costs of 0.0-0.5 against a `total_budget` of 1.5 make
  total-cost affordability vacuous. Do not re-run either.
- Raising the constant uniformly is also **refuted with a number**: with
  `LEXICAL_BRANCH_KEEP` at the full 160-wide shortlist, the release binary
  takes **20.0 s** for the canonical target (a 10x wall-clock regression,
  which this repository's own criterion forbids) and the clue is **still
  absent** ([w-6b2f04](w-6b2f04.md), integrated as `eaab259`). That 20 s was
  measured under the *current* best-first traversal, which pays roughly the
  product of the fan-outs; it is not a property of the width.
- The whole budget family is refuted: the global emission budget, the global
  pop budget, per-segmentation depth, per-slot width, and every allocation of
  them across 256 segmentations. `LEXICAL_GLOBAL_EMISSION_BUDGET`,
  `LEXICAL_GLOBAL_POP_BUDGET`, `structure_depth_ceiling` and
  `structure_wording_allowance` are now on the accumulation branch and are
  **not** to be edited by this front.

## The mechanism this front owns

The traversal, not the list length, should decide how deep a slot is read.

Concretely: keep each slot's full match list (it is already built and sorted
by `SlotAlt::contribution`), and let the existing per-segmentation emission
and pop accounting stop reading a slot once the traversal's budget cannot
use more of it. The uniform pre-filter is then replaced by a *reachability*
rule: a slot may be read deeply only when the traversal is actually spending
on that level, so a level that is never visited costs nothing, and a level
that is visited deeply is paid for out of the budget that already bounds it.

The general property to preserve, and to state in a test: **the truncation
must never be able to remove a candidate that the traversal would have
emitted**, and it must never cost more than the traversal it replaces. A
mechanism that keeps the full lists and relies on the traversal's bound is
preferred over any new constant.

### What landed

`LEXICAL_BRANCH_KEEP = 10` — the uniform truncation in front of the
per-segmentation product search — **no longer exists**. `src/lib.rs` now has
`LEXICAL_BRANCH_STAGE_0 = 10` and `LEXICAL_BRANCH_STAGE_GROWTH = 4`, and
`next_branch_stage(current, widest) -> Option<usize>` is the whole rule: the
walk opens every slot at 10, reads that width to exhaustion, and asks for the
next one (x4: 10 -> 40 -> 160, spanning `SPAN_SHORTLIST`) **only if it is
still hungry**. `None` means every alternative of the widest slot has been
read. The reserve's ladder is now written in multiples of the opening width
and is numerically unchanged: `[10, 30, 80, 200]`.

Nothing new is bounded by the rule and nothing old stopped bounding it: the
per-segmentation emission allowance, `LEXICAL_HEAP_POP_LIMIT`, and the two
global budgets all still apply, unchanged.

Two implementation facts, both measured rather than argued:

- **The bookkeeping is paid only when a widening happens.** The record of
  expanded nodes is written by a *replay* — a second pass over a freshly
  seeded heap at the *same* width, which records what it expands and emits
  nothing — and a replay is scheduled only after a walk has drained with
  appetite left. The pass that runs on every segmentation of every target
  allocates nothing per pop. A first attempt that recorded on every pop was
  wrong twice over: a replay at the *new* width re-walked the whole narrow
  lattice and tripped the pop limit, and a per-stage emission counter let one
  segmentation buy several allowances. With both fixed, the output is
  byte-identical to the single-pass version on all ten measured targets.
- **The remaining price:** a widening costs one extra pass over the narrow
  lattice. Measured fire counts (temporary counter, reverted;
  `git grep zz_` is empty): **0** on `it's just a stupid game`,
  `congratulations on your promotion`, `they have really good ideas`,
  `a whole lot of trouble`, `the old man lives here`; 3 on
  `recognize speech`, 1 on `I love you`, 1 on `sign on`, 2 on `wow`, 3 on
  `taco cat`, 3 on `big spender`, 6 on `play games with me now`.

### Composition with the reserve — measured, not assumed

The two spends share one per-segmentation allowance: the reserve takes up to
`EMIT_PROFILE_RESERVE = 16` of 64 first and the traversal is handed the
remainder, so they are not additive. Measured with the reserve's own
depth counters over the reachability corpus at `top_n 50`, before and after:

|---|---|---|---|
| traversal's deepest emitted slot index, before | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| traversal's deepest emitted slot index, after | **9** | **38** | **31** |

| reserve's wordings, before -> after | 3807 -> 3807 | 3681 -> 3681 | 938 -> 938 |

Three things follow, and they are the useful output of this pass:

1. **The reserve is not displaced.** Its emission count is identical to the
   unit on all three targets, and the traversal's total rises by exactly the
   number of its new deep wordings. The two spends share the allowance
   without cannibalising each other, because the reserve spends first and is
   capped at 16 whatever the traversal does with the remainder.
2. **The coupling is real and is bounded by the ladder's top working rung.**
   The traversal now crosses the reserve's first two rungs (10, 30) on two of
   three targets and does not reach the third (80). So the traversal does
   partly buy what the shallow rungs buy — and w-e07c42's own bar, which
   read `traversal < LEXICAL_BRANCH_KEEP`, was true only by construction and
   is now false. **That bar was changed, deliberately and visibly**: it is
   now `traversal < EMIT_DEEP_INDEX_LADDER[2]`, which asserts the front's
   actual purpose — the reserve still reaches a region the traversal does
   not. The old assertion, the reason it changed, and the numbers are in the
   doc comment of `depth_profile_emissions_reach_deeper_than_the_traversal`.
3. **On the canonical target the coupling is exactly zero.** The traversal's
   deepest index is 9 before and after, its deep-wording count is 0, and the
   reserve's 3807 are all doing work the traversal cannot do. Whatever blocks
   the canonical clue, it is not the reserve and not the width.

### Blockers, stated precisely

- **This front is not the milestone and does not claim to be.** The canonical
  clue is still absent, and the measurement above says the truncation was
  never the operative constraint on the targets that matter.
- **The residual is the traversal's order, not its width.** w-6b2f04
  measured full width at 17.2 s and still missing; w-8f3c61 refuted the
  order family in closed form; w-be6d21 measured that the global budgets are
  non-binding and that the pool saturates. Between them, no width change and
  no budget change can reach the node: reaching it needs a traversal that
  arrives at a deep node *within* `LEXICAL_HEAP_POP_LIMIT = 4_000` rather
  than one that stops at 64 emissions with better nodes still in the heap.
  This front removes the ceiling that would have blocked such a traversal
  from working, and measures that the ceiling was not what was stopping it.
- **Affordability caveat, unchanged and now sharper.** This branch is
  affordable *because* a widening almost never fires. That is a measured
  property of the current traversal, not of the mechanism. If the traversal
  changes such that walks drain more often, this branch's cost profile moves
  with it. The widening counter is the test, and it is the thing to re-measure
  first if the traversal changes.
