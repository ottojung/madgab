# Make the traversal's per-slot ORDER put a span's right word inside the opening width

## Goal

Establish whether `SlotAlt::contribution` — the ordering the traversal uses to place a span's
candidates inside the opening width — can be re-specified so that a faithful near-homophonic
resegmentation's needed word lands inside the affordable opening width, **without** losing the
descending-bound property that makes the emission order worth anything, and without buying more
budget than the search already spends.

## Why this is the open coordinate

[../REPORT-4d7c12.md](../REPORT-4d7c12.md), integrated at this item's base, is the first front to
**localise** the loss of the canonical case-2 tuple. Per-stage counts for
`it's just a stupid game` → `hits justice dupe hid came`:

| stage | count |
|---|---|
| 1 fuzzy per-span generation | 108 spans, 14,180 offered |
| 2 per-span shortlist | 9,877 retained |
| 3 structural DP | 1,854 paths, 256 kept |
| 4 slot/heap retention | 240 funded, opening widths {5, 7, 10} |
| 5 emissions | 2,929 reserve + 12,431 traversal + 1,024 adjacency = 16,384 |
| 6 `Partial` assembly | 16,384 |
| 7 dedup/pool | 18,949 |

The word multiset survives stages 2 and 3 (two chains retained, schedule ranks 151 and 187) and
**first becomes unrepresentable at stage 4**: opening width
`affordable_opening_width(5, LEXICAL_HEAP_POP_LIMIT) = 7` against needed per-slot indices
**`7 / 0 / 13 / 99 / 11`**, the deepest 14.1× the width. Stage 5 cannot recover it because the
reserve is *marginal by construction* (slot 1 absent from all 14/15 draws; largest funded subset 3
deep; the canonical needs 4).

The decisive observation is the green control: a cell **inside** the opening width is reachable
today, at zero extra cost, by the traversal — `wreck a nice beach` is emitted at tuples
`[1,0,0,2]` and `[1,0,2,2]` inside a cap of 10. The canonical would be reachable the same way if
the traversal's slot ordering put the right word of each of its five spans inside the first 7
positions. That is an **ordering** property, not a coverage one, and it is the only remaining
mechanism that needs no extra budget.
