# Re-specify the non-acoustic axes of the final score so they do not anti-correlate with the intended answer

## Goal

Produce and price a **general** revision to the final score's seven non-acoustic axes, or a new
axis alongside them, that makes a faithful near-homophonic resegmentation rank in the top 50 for
its own target, **without pushing the currently-green canonical case out of the list**.

The starting evidence is [../REPORT-6b2e19.md](../REPORT-6b2e19.md), integrated at `7e648fe`. Carry
its counterfactual table as this front's refutation baseline; anything that fails that table is
already priced and must not be re-tried.

## Why the existing answers are refuted (baseline, do not re-run these)

From `w-6b2e19` on integrated HEAD, pool of 18,949 retained candidates for
`it's just a stupid game` -> `hits justice dupe hid came`:

* case-2 canonical score **0.8207695329**, cutoff (50th) **0.9151215745**, gap **0.0943520415**;
* per-term attribution of that gap: **NOVELTY +0.0500 (53%)**, **FAMILIARITY +0.0252 (27%)**,
  **PUNCH +0.0200 (21%)**, **SIMILARITY +0.0033 (3.5%)**, CLOSED_CLASS −0.0042;
* the top 50 is **acoustically worse** than the pool mean: SIMILARITY lift **−0.0205**, while
  RHYTHM +0.2763, NOVELTY +0.1914, PUNCH +0.1526, FAMILIARITY +0.1221;
* `recognize speech` -> `wreck a nice beach` is green (pool rank 27, score 0.9199502875, displayed
  at 26/50, +0.0012 over the cutoff) **because of** FAMILIARITY (+0.0248), and is charged
  SIMILARITY −0.0143 against the cutoff;
* zeroing NOVELTY moves case 2 only 9,664 -> 8,212 and drops the green case 27 -> 125; zeroing
  FAMILIARITY moves case 2 to 8,760 and drops the green case 27 -> 370; zeroing all three reaches
  4,793; ranking on SIMILARITY alone puts case 2 at 12,499 and the green case at 3,428;
* case 2's tuple is **absent from the pool**, so enumeration is a prerequisite too
  (that surface is owned by the sibling front w-4d7c12; see *Independence*);
* MMR/diversity is not the cause.

So: **no monotone re-weighting of the existing axes can serve both cases.** The canonical cases sit
on opposite sides of NOVELTY and FAMILIARITY.

## What this front must do

1. **Reproduce the baseline cheaply.** Reuse the `#[cfg(test)]` decomposition technique from
   `w-6b2e19` (its branch `madgab-score-6b2e19` holds the instrumented `Metrics`: the eight
   weighted terms, the eight raw axes, `SCORED`/`drain_scored`, and a helper that prices an
   arbitrary word sequence by aligning it through the production fuzzy lattice). Cherry-pick or
   re-create that instrumentation **on your own branch**; it is test-only and must never be
   integrated as production source. Confirm you reproduce 0.8207695329 / rank 9,664 and the
   green case's 0.9199502875 / rank 27 before you trust any conclusion.
2. **Explain the two refuted axes as defects, not as preferences.** Show *why* NOVELTY charges a
   resegmentation for keeping target boundaries and *why* FAMILIARITY charges a tuple of rarer
   words, in terms of what the axis was presumably meant to reward. Name the property each axis
   gets backwards, using measured data over the whole pool, not intuition.
3. **Design at least two general candidate revisions** and price each one by re-ranking the
   captured pool (re-weighting measured raw values, or recomputing a proposed new axis over the
   captured candidates). Required properties for any candidate:
   * keyed only on measurable per-word, per-span or per-cut properties available in the library
     (edit costs, cut vector, syllable counts, closed-class membership, corpus frequency, and
     whatever else the report shows is measurable) - never on the sentence, the clue, the phrase,
     the target, or any literal token from either example;
   * it must **keep** `wreck a nice beach` inside the top 50 for `recognize speech` **and** move
     `hits justice dupe hid came` (priced at 0.8207695329, or at whatever it reaches once the
     enumeration front restores it to the pool) inside the top 50;
   * it must not simply invert an axis - if a candidate rule only helps by preferring rare or
     common words, or by preferring shared or destroyed boundaries, say that plainly and price it
     as refuted.
4. **Price a new-axis proposal** (the report's option (a)): an axis the canonical tuple saturates
   and the current head does not, with the axis's lift table over the pool printed before and
   after. Or price option (b) - that NOVELTY and FAMILIARITY are mis-specified - with the same
   evidence.
5. **Validate anything you implement**: `cargo test --release --lib` at 74 passed / 0 failed (with
   `approximate_finds_classic_madgab_resegmentation` the known pre-existing red, not re-pinned),
   `cargo test --release --test no_phrase_hard_coding` 9/9, and
   `cargo test --release --test corpus_integration`. Add a **red/green** regression test that

   9.5s / 21.3s from base, 12.5s/21.7s if you have a newer figure).
6. **Report explicitly** if your best candidate cannot clear 0.0944 without regressing the green
