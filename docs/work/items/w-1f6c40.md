### Validation of the instrumentation itself

`axis_breakdown` must reproduce the production score or the per-axis tables
mean nothing. The harness prints the largest absolute difference between the
recomposed total and `Clue::score` over the printed 50 plus the first 2000
pool entries. It is **0.000e0 in every run recorded below** — the
decomposition is bit-identical, not approximate.

Its limits are stated where they matter, not hidden: `axis_breakdown` on an
emitted clue is exact; `alignment_axes` on a *named* alignment reproduces the
search's word placement only where the search emits that alignment, and for
one it does not emit the placement is the harness's (see case 2 below).
