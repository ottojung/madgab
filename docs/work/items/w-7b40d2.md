# Cost-stratified shortlist fill: stop truncating the last 60 of every 160-wide shortlist by raw quality

## Goal

Make the shortlist **fill** respect the cost-band structure the selection rule already builds, so that
no span's cheapest words are dropped at **every** width for a reason unrelated to their cost. Keep
`SPAN_SHORTLIST = 160`: this changes *which* 160 candidates are retained, not how many, and adds no
scoring work.

## Why this is worth a front (the pricing is already done)

The band structure that exists precisely to prevent "the last slots of a full shortlist are the
worst-sounding retained words" is applied only to the first
`16 + 16 + 4 + 48 + 16 = 100` slots of a 160-wide shortlist. The remaining **60 slots (37.5%) are
an undifferentiated quality top-up carrying no band guarantee**, and because `quality` degrades
monotonically with phonetic distance, those 60 slots are systematically the *worst-sounding*
retained words. Measured over **939 span edges**: candidates within 1.5x of their span's cheapest —
the same threshold the acceptance reachability test uses — come to **7,056**, of which **623
(8.8%) are dropped at every width**; worst dropped quality rank **255**; worst per-span share
**24 of 56 (43%)** on the ɛ-initial one-phoneme spans (`[8,9)` of `in the middle of the night`,
`[4,5)` of `a whole lot of trouble`, `[11,12)` of `we should have told her`).

Full derivation, per-target tables and the rejected alternatives:
[w-0f3a17-shortlist-rule.md](w-0f3a17-shortlist-rule.md) and the "latent defect, priced" section of
[w-0f3a17](w-0f3a17.md). This item exists because that front priced the defect, deliberately did not
implement it (it is not the canonical blocker, and two pool-reach guards were re-pinned against this
selection rule), and then went terminal. The blocker on that rule has since been recorded: the guards
are green at `707fb2a` and the re-pinning risk is exactly what the fence below exists to catch.

## Independence from the w-9e2b41 fronts

`w-9e2b41` owns the **per-slot enumeration cap** (`affordable_opening_width` / `LEXICAL_BRANCH_STAGE_0`
and the traversal walk over `slots[k]`). This front owns the **contents of `selected`** for a span.
The shortlist is a pure function of the span's candidate set and the target — `cap`, `widest`,
`next_branch_stage` and every emission budget are **not inputs to it**. The two fronts therefore touch
different functions and must not be merged into one branch; the coordinator integrates in that order,
each behind its own fence.
