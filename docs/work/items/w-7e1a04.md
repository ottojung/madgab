# Case 2 is blocked by the objective, not by search: give the score an axis that sees a well-formed multiword resegmentation

## Goal

Make the itinerary's second canonical clue reachable by changing the *form* of
the clue score, in a way that is general and helps other inputs, so that
`madgab --approximate` proposes `Hits Justice Dupe Hid Came` for

## Why this item exists

Every search-side explanation for the second canonical clue has now been
measured and retired. [w-b3e91a](w-b3e91a.md) is the last of them and its
verdict, recorded on `b0c7d66`, is negative and priced:

* the global emission ceiling is **saturated** (16,384/16,384) and the pop
  ceiling is not (17-19%), so the "the ceiling leaves slack" premise behind
  the adjacency operator's funding was false and is now corrected and pinned
  by a test;
* at the **full** per-slot width of 160 the traversal emits 2,036,664 wordings
  of the canonical segmentation's own structure, reaches index 159, and still
  never emits the canonical tuple. The shortfall is a factor of **124** in the
  cheapest reallocation and **1,309** in the segmentation's
  2,666,496,000-wording product. It is an *order* limit, not a reach limit, so
  no emission allocation can fix it;
* 40 additive-adjacency configurations, and the whole depth-profile sampler,
  produce zero hits. The item's literal "emissions must reach deep indices"
  goal was already met and already asserted by the green
  `depth_profile_emissions_reach_deeper_than_the_traversal`;
* forced onto the unchanged production path, the canonical alignment scores
  **0.820770** at pool rank **9,668 of 18,937** against a printed-50 cutoff of
  **0.912971**: a gap of **+0.092201** and a rank deficit of 9,618 places.
  Emissions do not move a score, so **the binding blocker is the objective**.

So the remaining question for the second canonical clue is a single general
question about the score's *form*, and it is the question this item asks.

  Its one durable finding is the shape of the defect: 0.45 of 1.15 weight is
  **exactly constant across every showable candidate** on 10/10 targets, so
  the score is largely a constant offset plus a couple of discriminating axes.

## The hypothesis this item is opened on

A clue that is a genuine resegmentation of the target is, almost by
definition, made of *more, shorter* words than the target's own wording. The
score has several axes whose penalty grows with the clue's own length or
segment count while the thing being rewarded does not:

* `axes::NOVELTY` is a Jaccard distance over boundary cuts, and both
  `target_inner_count` and `clue_inner = depth - 1` grow with resegmentation,
  so a clue that re-cuts the target into more pieces is charged twice for the
  same evidence;
* `axes::WORD_NOVELTY` is `1 - reused / word_count`, so splitting the target's
  words up is charged as novelty even when the resegmentation is faithful;
* `axes::SIMILARITY` normalises cost by the target's phone count, which is
  length-neutral, while the per-word proxies normalise by word count, which
  is not.

The canonical alignment's recorded loss decomposition is consistent with this:
it loses on acoustic cost, boundary novelty and familiarity and wins only on
the weak axes. Whether that is a length artefact or a real quality gap is
exactly what this item must settle **by measurement on many targets, not on
the canonical pair alone**.

## Constraints and traps this repository has already paid for

* Measurement harnesses that silently change the quantity being measured have
  corrupted this work before: a harness computing `combined` as `iter().sum()`
  changed the pool from 18,065 to 20,064. Validate any probe by reproducing
  the pristine pool count and the pre-existing `MADGAB_TRACE` cutoff to nine
  decimals before believing a number from it.
* `cargo` reuses stale binaries across `git bisect`; force a rebuild or the
  "first bad commit" will be a docs commit.
* No `ZZ_*`/`zz_*` probe instrumentation, no `zz_dump_canon`-style survey test
  and no temporary `#[cfg(test)]` counters may reach the accumulation branch.
  Probe work belongs on a separate `scratch/` branch in its own worktree.
* Commit **and push** implementation work before spending further turns
  measuring. Uncommitted work in a worktree is unrecoverable from a later
  pass's point of view, and a finished agent on a detached HEAD is worse.
* Finish on a named branch, and check `git branch --contains <head>` before
  treating the branch as safe.
