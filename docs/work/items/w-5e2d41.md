# Cheap-end floor plus band apportionment in the span shortlist fill: keep the 623 at zero without turning the pool-reach guard red

## Goal

Land [w-7b40d2](w-7b40d2.md) (branch `madgab-fillstrat-7b40d2`, `a8a8f70`) with its one red fence green
instead of held, by replacing the **pure** population-proportional apportionment with the general rule
its own record derives: **retain at least as many candidates at or below each cheap cost threshold as
the global `quality` order would have retained, then apportion the remaining slots over the bands.**

The rule is keyed on measured properties only - candidate cost, band population, `quality`, slot
count. No sentence, clue or word list. Nothing about `hid`, `net`, `taco cat` or any other literal
may appear in the rule; those are the *measurements* that sized it, not inputs to it.

## Why this is worth a front

`a8a8f70` already closes the priced defect outright - cheap candidates dropped at every width
**623 -> 0**, worst per-span share **43% -> 0**, unrepresented non-empty cost bands **1 -> 0** of
3,234 - with the printed top-50 of both canonical cases **bit-identical** and `--test corpus_integration`
at exactly the pristine baseline (12 passed / 1 failed, the pre-existing
`approximate_finds_classic_madgab_resegmentation`). It is held only because
`--test emit_coverage` is 3 passed / 1 failed: `a_lattice_alignment_can_be_absent_from_the_production_pool`
goes red, `"hid" is absent from the pool entirely`, and the front's own measured attribution is that
all five canonical words are still in the shortlist of the span each lands on, four of five still in
the pool - so the loss is **downstream of the selection rule**, in the 16,384 global emissions, caused
by a tighter `min_cost` and a changed branch composition.

Its section 6.2 gives the arithmetic that a pure repartition is *not* viable and that a floor is:
on `taco cat` span `[6,8)` the band populations are `[3, 44, 152, 57]`, `net` sits in band 2 at cost
0.2649745686 with 47 candidates cheaper, and a pure proportional fill of 60 slots gives band 2 only
`60 * 152/256 = 35.6`, dropping any band-2 word outside band 2's own quality top ~36. The cheap bands
are already safe on that span (3/3 and 44/44 retained in full). So the shortfall is bounded by the
**thickest** band, not the cheapest, and the cheap end is where the floor must be stated.
