# Localise the exact pipeline stage that loses the canonical case-2 tuple, and price a general coverage rule

## Goal

Establish by measurement **which stage of the approximate pipeline first destroys the joint
configuration** that would produce `Hits Justice Dupe Hid Came` for `It's just a stupid game`, and
price the one **general** coverage rule that would keep that stage's losses from pruning faithful
resegmentations - for this input and for others.

## The finding this front exists to exploit

[../REPORT-6b2e19.md](../REPORT-6b2e19.md), integrated at `7e648fe`, established on integrated
HEAD that the canonical case-2 tuple is **not a member of the retained pool at all**. It is absent
from all 18,949 candidates that `generate_pool` returns *after* the search's own dedup and
retention, while the sequence is known to be reachable in the fuzzy lattice inside the default
budgets (`acceptance_sequences_are_reachable_in_fuzzy_lattice` in `src/lib.rs`).

Per-word pool membership, also from that report:

| word | pool members containing it |
|---|---|
| `hits` | 19 |
| `justice` | 10,044 |
| `dupe` | 30 |
| `hid` | **2** |
| `came` | 138 |

Its least-cost alignment is at cut offsets [3, 10, 13, 15, 19] of the 19-phone target with per-word
costs `hits 0.20, justice 0.00, dupe 0.15, hid 0.3695, came 0.40` (total 1.1195), well inside the
1.5 budget. So the tuple is affordable and reachable, and it is still never built.

Note the shape of the evidence: `hid` is nearly absent from the pool on its own, so this is very
likely **not** a per-word ranking problem but a per-span or joint-coverage problem - the same
marginal-versus-joint wall the closed fronts found from the other side. The earlier fronts priced
the *budget* (`w-9e2b41`: `LEXICAL_HEAP_POP_LIMIT` is charged jointly for the whole width vector),
the *depth cap* (`w-c3f81a`: `funded_slot_depth` replaced `EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP=3` at zero cost),
the *shortlist fill* (`w-7b40d2`) and the *cheap-end retention floor* (`w-5e2d41`, front A) - all
priced negative. **None of them localised the loss.** This front does not buy more width; it finds
the stage.
