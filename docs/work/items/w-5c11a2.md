# Re-measure the canonical case-2 objective gap on the merged tree, then
# attack the *relative* ranking of equally well-scored long clues

## Why this item exists

Two independent blockers stand between the current
`post-milestone-acceptance` and the milestone's second canonical case, and as
of `f1943fd` exactly one of them is priced against the current tree.

1. **Emission.** The alignment is absent from the production deduplicated
   candidate pool (0 of 18,824), *not merely from the printed 50*. Priced and
   accepted as a negative result in [w-7c1f64](w-7c1f64.md): the search is
   emission-bound, not pop-bound, and widening the traversal makes it
   strictly worse.
2. **Objective ranking.** Forcing the tuple onto the *unchanged* production
   path, the real scorer puts it at **0.7999**, **pool rank 9,699 of 18,825**,
   against a **rank-50 cutoff of 0.895692** — a gap of **+0.0958**.

Blocker 2's number was measured on the tree **before** the per-word
`SIMILARITY` axis from [w-9c6f2b](w-9c6f2b.md) was integrated. That axis was
predicted to be worth up to `+0.0700` on this candidate, and
[w-9c6f2b](w-9c6f2b.md)'s own caveat is that the *absolute* figure is not the
criterion — the rank-50 cutoff is relative, and lifting similarity for every
long clue lifts the whole distribution, so the cutoff rises too. **The
integrated tree has therefore never had this gap measured on it.** Every
cutoff and rank figure in [w-7c1f64](w-7c1f64.md) and [w-9c6f2b](w-9c6f2b.md)
is a baseline on a pre-integration tree and is stale for the purpose of
deciding what happens next.

So the first deliverable is a number, and the second is the change that
number justifies.

## Goal

On the current `post-milestone-acceptance` tree, establish by measurement —
not by re-derivation — (a) whether the alignment is now *in* the pool,
(b) its pool rank and score if it is, and (c) the current rank-50 cutoff, and
then, if it still ranks out, remove the *general* reason it is out-ranked
among otherwise comparably-scored long clues.
