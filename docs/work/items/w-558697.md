# The residual is in the objective, not the enumeration: design and measure candidate *new* scoring axes

## Goal

Find the next general degree of freedom in approximate clue scoring, with evidence,
as a **report**. After this item the repository should know which new axis (if any)
is worth landing, on the strength of a mechanism argument plus per-target
measurements — not on the strength of the fact that it lifts one clue.

This is the honest next move named by [w-5c8e1f](w-5c8e1f.md)'s handoff once M7a
lands, and it is deliberately **investigation only**: nothing may be landed here.

## Why the search side is closed, and why the objective is what remains

Both halves of the search side have been closed by measurement, on 12-13 targets,
by fronts whose reports are durable on `origin`:

- `c81e55` (`git show madgab-depthcap-c81e55:REPORT-c81e55.md`, pushed `b8391f6`)
  §4: the search-side mechanisms M1-M6 are **zero-effect** on this milestone.
  §2: the depth cap `EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP = 3` (`src/lib.rs:212`) is **inert** for
  the shapes that carry the requested wording, which are 5-slot segmentations.
  §3: pushing the requested index tuples through the ordinary `build` with the
  ordinary scorer moves the wording from absent to **9867 of 17 908** — a gap of
  0.0958, about 11x the width of the visible top-50 band.
  §5: **no reweighting of the six existing axes reaches the cutoff**; the only one
  that does deletes FAMILIARITY and cuts NOVELTY, moving 21.3 % of the scoring mass
  into RHYTHM, which works purely because the requested clue maxes RHYTHM. That is
  forbidden (see below), and §5 is also the reason weights are not the answer.
- `b47d02` (`git show madgab-depth4-b47d02:REPORT-b47d02.md`, pushed `b8aa6d8`) §1.1:
  cap 4 offers depth-4 zero times in 45 387 offers across 12 targets; 79 % of
  four-deep tuples are refused by the additive total budget, and the reserve samples
  ~0.001 % of the four-deep rectangle, so 1 085 offers at the right slot set produce
  the requested word 0 times.

Axis *weights* were additionally closed as a family by [w-3f9c02](w-3f9c02.md): the
residual is not a weighting problem. [w-2e5b93](w-2e5b93.md) and
[w-9c2d51](w-9c2d51.md) closed the spread and content-word-prior families.

So the remaining general degrees of freedom are *new axes with an independent
argument*. The question this item answers is **which one**, if any.

## The question the front must answer

For each candidate axis, in one table:

1. **The mechanism**, in terms of what a Mad Gab audience is responding to when they
   accept a proposal that the current objective under-rewards. If the mechanism is
   "this helps the acceptance clue", it is not a mechanism: reject the candidate and
   say so in one line.
2. **Does it help inputs that are not the acceptance examples?** Measured, not
   asserted: the axis's per-target effect on at least six ordinary multi-word targets
   (`recognize speech` counts as ordinary here; the canonical target is *not* the
   only target that matters and is not a reason).
3. **Cost and interaction**: what it costs in wall clock, whether it can be computed
   from information the scorer already has (no new traversal pass), and what it does
   to the other axes' spread.
4. **Whether it closes the gap**, and if not, by how much it moves the requested
   wording's rank out of 17 908 toward the top-50 band, measured with the
   **production** scorer (`MADGAB_TRACE_PHRASES`, or the injection discipline
   `c81e55` used) — never by a probe-only scoring path.

A candidate that only helps at rank ~1101 of 3 050 is a result worth recording, not
a proposal to land. Say so.

## Forbidden, explicitly

  `madgab-axis-558697`. No `src/` change, no test change, no constant. A later work
  item carries the landing, and only on a report that survives review.
- **No reweighting** of the six existing axes. `c81e55` §5 measured that family to be
  both ineffective and, in the one variant that works, a re-pointing of the objective
  at one clue. It is forbidden here, not merely deprioritised.

  environment-value case**, in the report's proposals or anywhere else. An axis

  failure mode this repository has fenced against since
  [w-d4f0b2](w-d4f0b2.md); `tests/no_phrase_hard_coding` must stay green.
