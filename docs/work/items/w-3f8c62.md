# Land the word-count parsimony axis in the structural keys as well as the final scorer

## Goal

Ship the one general objective repair that three consecutive fronts have independently identified
and priced, and that no front was allowed to ship: a **word-count parsimony** term
`1 - |w_clue - w_target| / max(w_clue, w_target)` at weight **0.15**, funded by `SHAPE` 0.05 →
0, `NOVELTY` 0.15 → 0.05 and `RHYTHM` 0.30 → 0.25 (`SIMILARITY` stays 0.25), added to the
**final scorer and to `complete_span_score`, `partial_span_score` and `span_score_bound`
together** so `structural_bounds_dominate_the_real_scorer` stays true.

The property this buys is general and phrase-free: *for a fixed target, the proposal set the
executable returns is at least as acoustically good as the pool it was drawn from.* It is
**currently false** — see the red fence `head_not_worse_than_pool` in `src/lib.rs`, integrated at
`515f8bd`, `#[ignore]`d and red on HEAD: 2 of 10 ordinary targets sit below their pool mean
(`they ate the whole pie` −0.0080, `my brother lost his wallet` −0.0184). Turning that fence
green is this item's success criterion, expressed at the library boundary with no sentence, no
clue and no exact rank.

This is a **general search-quality** item, not a case-2 reach item. `REPORT-3a8c05.md` §3's
weight-free bound of rank 1,127 is a floor under *every* monotone objective, so nothing here
reaches the canonical case-2 tuple and nothing here may claim to.
