# Deliver the canonical Mad Gab resegmentations from approximate mode

## Goal

`madgab --approximate` should propose `wreck a nice beach` for
`recognize speech` and `Hits Justice Dupe Hid Came` for
`It's just a stupid game`, as a result of general improvements to
search, segmentation, scoring and ranking — never phrase-specific
hard-coding.

## Context

The itinerary's primary goal is
[../skills/itinerary-madgab.md](../skills/itinerary-madgab.md). The two
regression tests in `tests/corpus_integration.rs`
(`approximate_finds_recognize_speech_resegmentation`,
`approximate_finds_classic_madgab_resegmentation`) have never passed on
this branch.

Measured starting point on `post-milestone-acceptance` (commit 247404f):

- both desired word sequences are already reachable in the fuzzy matcher
  lattice inside the default budgets; `acceptance_sequences_are_reachable_in_fuzzy_lattice`
  in `src/lib.rs` proves it;
- `wreck a nice beach` was generated at raw rank 662 of 14068
  candidates, score 0.8946, against a 0.9063 cutoff — a ranking problem;
- `hits justice dupe hid came` was **not generated at all**; its
  best alignment costs 1.12 (hits 0.20, justice 0.00, dupe 0.15,
  hid 0.37, came 0.40) inside the 1.5 total budget, but the word `hid`
  was pruned out of the retained shortlist for its target span, and the
  segmentation itself did not survive the structural DP;
- the visible top-50 was pathologically redundant: for
  `It's just a stupid game`, 27 of the 50 proposals shared one boundary
  structure, because `select_diverse` scaled its redundancy penalty by
  the score spread *inside the requested top-N* (a few thousandths), and
  `dedup` treated `this` and `this'` as different clues;
- wall clock was 9.5s for `recognize speech` and 21.3s for
  `It's just a stupid game`, 48.5s for
  `congratulations on your promotion`.

## Completion criteria

- Both canonical clues appear in the approximate proposal set for their
  own target, with the rank chosen by evidence rather than asserted
  arbitrarily; a regression test states the boundary that actually
  matters and the reasoning.
- The implementation contains no phrase-specific special case for these
  examples, their words, or any substring of them.
- The proposal set is not redundant: proposals for one target span
  several distinct boundary structures, covered by a general test.
- `cargo test --release` is green.
- Search wall clock does not regress against the figures above.
- Work is committed on a focused branch and integrated into
  `post-milestone-acceptance`, never into `main`.

## Handoff / notes

Branch `madgab-approx-acceptance` (commit 6250ba3) lands the first slice,
by Antonina agent `a1b2c301`:

- the score's raw-IPA-length proxy is replaced by a syllable-count
  *rhythm* axis (weight 0.30), so the search no longer wins by shredding
  the target into one- and two-phone words;
- clue identity is now orthography-independent (`phrase_signature`), so
  spelling variants of one clue collapse;
- `select_diverse` measures its penalty against the whole candidate
  pool, maximises redundancy over everything picked rather than only the
  previous pick, and adds a 0.5-weighted boundary-cut Jaccard term;
- the per-segmentation lexical enumeration became a best-first search
  over prefixes with admissible suffix bounds, so emission order really
  is descending in final score;
- the per-span shortlist gained a least-familiar-candidate axis and a
  160-word fill, and segmentation retention doubled to 256;
- beam retention is now deterministic (deduped candidates sorted by path
  key, cell keys sorted) — `HashMap` iteration order had been leaking
  into results;
- `boundary_novelty` counts by merge instead of building `HashSet`s, and
  the target-word reuse test is memoised.

Measured result: `wreck a nice beach` raw rank 662 -> 52
(score 0.9277 against a 0.9279 cutoff) and *not* in the visible top 120
either, because MMR keeps at most one clue per boundary structure and two
siblings of the same structure already outscore it; top-50 structural
redundancy is gone; search is 3-5x faster
(`congratulations on your promotion` 48.5s -> 11.5s).

Residual, and the reason this item is still `working`:

- `hits justice dupe hid came` is still absent from the retained pool.
  It scores 0.820 against a 0.926 cutoff: it loses on acoustic cost
  (-0.057), boundary novelty (-0.050) and familiarity (-0.030) and wins
  only on the two weak axes, so no monotone reweighting of the current
  axes can surface it.
- The current winners are function-word salads — `it justice two bad
  aim`, `wreck a nice each`, `so la cut are -able` — that no human would
  use as a puzzle. A closed-class / content-word prior on clue quality is
  the honest next axis, and it is expected to help both canonical cases.
  Tracked as [w-9c2d51](w-9c2d51.md).
