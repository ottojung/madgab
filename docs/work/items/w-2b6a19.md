# Lexicon coverage: is the case-2 answer expressible at all at the default rarity bound?

## Goal

Establish, by measurement rather than inference, whether `hits justice dupe hid came` is
*representable* in the candidate vocabulary that `madgab --approximate` searches at its default
configuration, and if any part of it is not, decide the general rule that fixes it.

This is the one surface upstream of search. Every other named case-2 surface is now closed or
priced: objective and admissible weights (`w-e086cc`), the coverage-reserve axis (`w-1a4e8d`),
adjacent-slot charging (`w-3f6a21`), span-shortlist retention (`w-5c1a3e`), and the
`prune_partials` discard threshold (`w-3c5b18`, priced negative, integrated at this pass). All of
them measure *ranks* and *values* of partials that already exist. None of them can say whether
the words the answer is made of were ever in the pool.

## Context and references

- The gate is `GeneratorConfig::max_rarity`, `src/lib.rs:76`, defaulting to `Some(50_000.0)` at
  `src/lib.rs:86`. It is consumed twice, at `src/lib.rs:806` by `Corpus::from_json` and at
  `src/lib.rs:815` by `approx::build_lexicon`, and is exposed on the CLI as `--max-rarity`
  (`src/main.rs:34`, `src/main.rs:74`). So the same bound can remove a word from the exact corpus,
  from the fuzzy lexicon, or from both, and the two consumers are not obviously in step.
- `dupe` is the word most at risk: it is a spelling, not a dictionary headword in the usual sense,
  and frequency-rank data is sparse for such forms. `hits` and `hid` are homograph-heavy and are
  at risk of a *sense* problem rather than a presence problem. Do not assume which; measure.
- The corpus is a vendored JSON loaded by the crate (no external data file), so a membership check
  is a library-level or executable-level fact, reproducible on this host.
- `src/lexical.rs:302` lists these exact words in a test asserting they are **not** closed-class
  words. That is a different property from corpus membership and is not evidence either way.
- Umbrella item: [w-4b1e07](w-4b1e07.md). Sibling live front on a disjoint surface:
  [w-8f0b3d](w-8f0b3d.md) (`agent-8f0b3d1`), which sweeps `--top` and budget knobs on the shipped
  binary and does **not** touch `--max-rarity` or lexicon construction.

## Why this is a real surface and not a duplicate

If any of the five clue words is filtered out at the default rarity bound, then every rank
measured upstream is a rank within a vocabulary that cannot express the answer, and the correct
next lever is the rarity bound's *character* (per-word, banded, or lexeme-expanded) rather than
anything inside the beam. If all five are present, that surface closes too and the durable result
is a one-paragraph negative that stops a future front from re-deriving it. Both outcomes are
useful; only measuring gives either.

## Completion criteria

1. A membership table for the five clue words, at the default `max_rarity` and at an unfiltered
   configuration, for **both** consumers: `Corpus::from_json` and `approx::build_lexicon`. Report
   the numbers, not a summary adjective.
2. If and only if a word is missing by default: a priced account of the general change that admits
   it, expressed as vocabulary/pronunciation/lexicon behaviour that helps other inputs, with its
   cost in pool size, wall clock and expansions.
3. A regression test at the library or executable boundary that pins the resulting property. It
   must not special-case the phrase, and the phrase literal must live in `tests/` or a
   `#[cfg(test)]` module so the `no_phrase_hard_coding` fence stays at 9/0 with `src/` at zero
   allowlist entries.
4. No change to objective weights, the score function, the selection rule, or `src/approx.rs`
   search behaviour. If the investigation concludes the surface is closed, the deliverable is
   `docs/work/REPORT-2b6a19.md` and no production change, and that is a success, not a failure.
5. Case 1 stays at or better than **display rank 27** in the shipped binary after any change. This
   is the standing guard; re-measure it, do not assume it.
