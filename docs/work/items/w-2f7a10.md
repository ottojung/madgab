# The canonical wording is enumerated nowhere in its own boundary structure:
# make the traversal reach a *pairing*, not just a single deep word

## Goal

Make the approximate mode generate the requested resegmentation for
`It's just a stupid game` as a result of general search, segmentation or
ranking behaviour — never a phrase-specific hard-code. The quantity that must
move is **whether the five-word pairing is enumerated at all**, and then whether
the selector admits it.

## Why this is a new item and not a continuation

[w-c4e8d7](w-c4e8d7.md) closed with its blocker list empty and, by its own
sequencing rule, stopped: membership went from 0 to non-zero and the wording is
still invisible, which that item defines as its successful end state. It also
forbids re-litigating selection, admission order, share cap and axis weights,
and [w-8a1d47](w-8a1d47.md) and [w-3f9c02](w-3f9c02.md) closed those two
territories. This item is what is left, and it is a **different question** from
either of them: not "which candidates are admitted" and not "how are they
scored", but "the traversal never emits this combination of coordinates at
all".

## The measurement this is based on

From `c4e8d70`'s end-state note on `da9c14c`, on the integrated tree
(`58a45b0` and after), release build, `--approximate --top 50`, offsets read
from `madgab::Clue::cuts`:

```text
It's just a stupid game, pool 17,907, 333 distinct structures,
visible cutoff 0.915691888 at rank 49

canonical structure [3, 10, 13, 15, 19]
  members in the pool                47
  best member                        "each justice too add gain"
  best-member global rank            3108
  best member score                  0.87812846738654216
  gap to the visible cutoff          -0.037563
  fill rank                          104   (the 50th visible slot is pool rank 104)
  the requested wording              not among the 47
```

The structure exists, is populated, and is reachable in the pool; the requested
wording is simply not one of the 47 wordings the traversal emits for it. So
**no scoring, admission, share-cap or cutoff change can surface it**, and the
best member's score is byte-identical to the parent's, which confirms the
reserve changed *which wordings are enumerated* and not how any of them scores.
That is the seam this item owns.

The single-word half of the problem is already solved and must stay solved: the
middle word of the requested alignment fills 14 of 17,907 candidates, and
`tests/corpus_integration.rs::approximate_pool_reaches_alternatives_past_the_opening_slot_width`
is the guard for it, as is
`approximate_finds_recognize_speech_resegmentation` for the first canonical
example.

## Hypotheses worth testing first, in order

1. **The per-slot depth profile is the constraint.** The reserve now walks slot
   subsets breadth-before-depth, so a word *is* reachable in each slot
   individually, but the emitted tuples may never place two deep coordinates in
   the *same* structure. A pairing needs more than one slot off the traversal's
   best index at once. `coverage_tuples` already has an `EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP`
   of 3 and subsets up to size 3; the question is whether the budget
   (`EMIT_PROFILE_RESERVE`) or the ordering is what keeps the requested shape
   out, and which.
2. **The cost bound rejects the tuple, not the ordering.** `build` compares each
   tuple's total substitution cost against `total_budget` additively. If the
   requested tuple's cost exceeds the budget while each of its words
   individually does not, then the per-slot affordability slack
   `sum_{j != k} m_j + cost_{k,i} <= total_budget` — the arithmetic
   [w-4b1e07](w-4b1e07.md)'s 11:52Z pass derived — is the real gate, and the
   fix is to make the *pair's* affordability visible to the traversal rather
   than each word's.
3. **The structure's own retention is the gate.** 47 members is small; check
   whether `SEGMENTATION_KEEP` or the pool's per-structure cap is what holds it
   at 47, and whether a structure that is *under*-represented is being
   crowded out of the pool by structures with more members.

## Scope and collision boundary

This front owns the traversal's **tuple space**: `coverage_tuples`,
`sweep_index`, `EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP`, `EMIT_PROFILE_RESERVE`,
`LEXICAL_COMBINATIONS_PER_SEGMENTATION`, the per-slot candidate lists, the
emission budget accounting in `build`, and the per-structure retention in the
pool. It may read `axes::*` and `select_diverse` to *diagnose* but must not
change them.

Forbidden: sweeping or re-tuning `axes::*` weights or any `boundary_novelty`
form (closed by [w-3f9c02](w-3f9c02.md)), `src/adjacency.rs` and
`ADJACENCY_*`, changing `select_diverse`'s admission order or the share cap /
`STRUCTURE_FLOOR` (closed by [w-8a1d47](w-8a1d47.md)), landing the parked
coverage-first patch, relaxing or re-baselining **either** acceptance test, and
any phrase-specific case. `approximate_output_is_locked` may be re-baselined
only with a record of which structures moved, and only as a consequence of this
item's own change.

## Completion criteria

1. **Diagnosis by line, before any fix.** The exact place in the default path
   where the requested tuple is not emitted: the function, the line, and the
   value of the quantity that rejects it. If the answer is "the traversal never
   offers that coordinate combination", show the count of tuples it does offer
   for that structure and the count it would need to.
2. **A landed, general fix** for the class, argued from the mechanism and never
   from either phrase: no special case for any word, substring or word sequence
   of either acceptance example, in `src/` or `tests/`.
3. **Measured membership, on the default path, release build:** the count of
   pool candidates containing the requested wording goes from **0** to
   non-zero, and the same count is reported for six real targets so the change
   is shown not to be specific to one input.
4. **A regression test** expressing the general property at an appropriate
   boundary — that a *pairing* of deep alternatives in different slots is
   reachable in the approximate pool, not merely each alternative alone — and
   naming neither acceptance phrase.
5. `approximate_finds_recognize_speech_resegmentation` and
   `approximate_pool_reaches_alternatives_past_the_opening_slot_width` both stay
   green. They are the guards, not targets.
6. `cargo test --release --lib`, `--test corpus_integration`,
   `--test exact_determinism`, `--test approx_determinism` and
   `--test no_phrase_hard_coding` reported, with `approx_determinism` and the
   fence green. `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy` and doctests do not exist on this
   host; see [../../environment-notes.md](../../environment-notes.md). Do not
   claim them.
7. Wall clock on `--approximate --top 50` not regressed. The 1.77-2.51 s column
   in [w-7b2d40](w-7b2d40.md) is **host noise** — `c4e8d70` measured the parent
   tree at 1.82-3.21 s in the same session — so measure interleaved against the
   parent in one session and say so, rather than quoting the old column. If
   deeper enumeration needs budget, **derive** it from the bound the search
   already respects (`total_budget`, compared additively at emission time)
   rather than raising a constant, and say what the constant was bounding.
