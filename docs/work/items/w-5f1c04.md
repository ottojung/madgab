# Make the global emission budget a real ceiling instead of the product of two per-segmentation constants

## Goal

The search's *global* emission budget is currently defined as
`SEGMENTATION_KEEP * LEXICAL_COMBINATIONS_PER_SEGMENTATION`, and the
per-segmentation allowance is clamped to `LEXICAL_COMBINATIONS_PER_SEGMENTATION`.
Those two facts together make the global budget **unreachable by
construction**: the whole search can never spend more than the sum of the
per-segmentation clamps, so the global constant is already at its maximum
reachable value. Decouple them — let the global budget be an independent
ceiling that a few boundary structures can be funded deeply into, while the
rest are funded at the current shallow allowance — so that a wording which is
jointly expensive but reachable inside a few thousand emissions of *one*
segmentation can enter the pool.

This is the "grow the budget ~8x" lever that
[w-8f3c61](w-8f3c61.md) §8.3 named, measured against and explicitly refused
to take, because it is a budget decision that belongs to the coordinator. This
item is that decision, taken on the evidence rather than on a ratio.

## Why this and not something else: the arithmetic, already measured

- [w-8f3c61](w-8f3c61.md) §8, from a confirmed build: the canonical
  resegmentation needs **>= 404,081 emissions** of its own segmentation to be
  emitted by cost-best-first order, and the cheapest depth-profile
  stratification is bounded below by **134,400 emissions**, against a
  per-segmentation allowance of **64** and a whole-search global emission
  budget of **16,384**.
- [w-3e5c7](w-3e5c7.md), integrated as `24f3cf7`: with the canonical
  alignment forced to index 0 of all five slots and that one segmentation's
  caps lifted, it sits at **emission 23,962 and pop 28,930 of its own
  segmentation**, at structural rank 151 of 256. The forced run *did* reach
  the clue. So a few tens of thousands of emissions inside one segmentation
  is sufficient, and it is ~375x the current per-segmentation allowance.
- `LEXICAL_GLOBAL_POP_BUDGET = 256 * 4_000 = 1,024,000` pops, and
  `28,930 < 1,024,000`. **The pop budget can already pay for it.** The only
  number that cannot is the emission budget, at 16,384, and it cannot be
  raised by any multiplier because it is defined as the product of the clamp
  that limits the spend.
- [w-be6d21](w-be6d21.md), integrated as `89c85ff`: the global
  emission/pop budget is **inert across 1x..256x** — the same pool, the same
  cutoff, the same verdict on five targets — and the pool saturates at
  177,029 by 64x. That sweep multiplied a number that could not be spent. Its
  handoff also records that the shipped constants are correct and provably
  non-binding *as they are written*, which is exactly the claim this item
  falsifies by changing the definition rather than the value.
- [w-6b2f04](w-6b2f04.md), integrated as `eaab259`, already put the
  structure-aware allocation machinery on the branch:
  `structure_wording_allowance(top_n)`, `structure_depth_ceiling(...)` and
  `funded: HashMap<structure, usize>`. This item reuses that machinery and
  changes what it is allowed to spend.

## What is closed, do not re-run it

Selection rule, visit order, per-slot width, per-slot cap, global
redistribution across structures at a fixed total, truncation point,
shortlist reordering, and the matcher's cost model are all refuted by
measurement. See [w-4b1e07](w-4b1e07.md) for the full list with numbers, and
do not open a sixth enumeration front. In particular:

- **do not** re-run a `1x..Nx` multiplier sweep of the *product*;
- **do not** touch the per-slot width schedule, which
  [w-9d4e17](w-9d4e17.md) owns and which is integrated as of `4c2200a`;
- **do not** touch the fuzzy matcher's cost model — see
  [w-3b8e15](w-3b8e15.md), where a rebuilt articulatory-feature cost lowered
  the canonical clue's price by 3.07x and changed nothing, because it
  rescaled every candidate equally instead of discriminating, and it
  regressed `wreck a nice beach` out of the pool.

## Mechanism sketch (yours to refine, not to copy blindly)

`emit_allowance` is currently `depth_ceiling.clamp(1, LEXICAL_COMBINATIONS_PER_SEGMENTATION)`.
The clamp is what makes the global constant inert. A general replacement:

1. give the global emission budget its own value, no longer written as
   `SEGMENTATION_KEEP * LEXICAL_COMBINATIONS_PER_SEGMENTATION`, and state in a
   comment what the search's real worst case is;
2. allow a *small number* of boundary structures to be funded far deeper than
   64 emissions, taken from the same global ceiling, with the rest funded at
   the current allowance. Which structures earn depth must come from a
   general, observable property — the selector admits a candidate from
   anywhere in the pool when its structure is unrepresented or under its
   `share_cap`, so under-represented structures are the ones worth buying —
   and never from anything about a particular target or phrase;
3. keep every bound bounded and named, and keep the pop budget as the
   independent wall-clock guard it already is.

If the emission ceiling turns out not to be the binding wall after all, say so
with numbers and close the item; that is a first-class result here.
