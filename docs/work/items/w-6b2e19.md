# Decompose the final-score margin between the canonical case-2 tuple and the 50th-best clue

## Goal

Establish, by measurement on the integrated accumulation branch, **which final-score component
accounts for the margin that keeps `It's just a stupid game` -> `hits justice dupe hid came` out of
the top-50 proposal set**, and state the one general change to that component that would close the
margin for this input *and* for other inputs.

This is a **read-only measurement and attribution front**. It may add `#[cfg(test)]`-gated
instrumentation on its own branch and it may write a `docs/work/REPORT-6b2e19.md`, but the deliverable
is a decomposition, not a production rule. Implementation, if the decomposition justifies one, is a
successor front.

## Why this is a new, independent front

Every front now closed on the acceptance blocker has bracketed the **search/enumeration** surface and
priced it negative from different sides:

* `w-9e2b41` - `LEXICAL_HEAP_POP_LIMIT` is charged **jointly** for the whole width vector, so a
  per-slot width table is a per-slot marginal and is infeasible at depth 5 (canonical tuple's own
  minimum sub-lattice costs 2.83x). Priced negative, both fronts agree.
* `w-c3f81a` - the depth cap was never the reason; `funded_slot_depth` replaced
  `EMIT_PROFILE_MAX_DEEP=3` at zero emission cost, and the residual finding is that the coverage
  reserve sweeps each marginal index at a uniform stride with essentially zero **joint** coverage.
  Integrated and closed.
* `w-7b40d2` / `w-5e2d41` - the shortlist fill and the cheap-end retention floor. Both priced
  negative; `hid` is lost to the **shortlist of one span**, not to `min_cost`, branch composition, or
  the 16,384-emission budget (7,312 emissions unspent).
* `agent-a1f3d2` (live, `w-5e2d41` surface) - re-widening the per-slot allocation does not turn the
  guard green either; its own run leaves the canonical count at 0.

So the search side has been priced from four directions and the blocker has not moved. That is the
signal to stop buying search width and **measure the ranking side instead**.

## The lead this front is opened on

`agent-a1f3d2`'s in-flight pool dump, read from its log at 09:52Z (a lead, not an established
result - this front exists to confirm or refute it independently on integrated HEAD):

* the production pool holds **22,784** deduped clues;
* the canonical case-2 tuple scores **0.8207695329** by the final scorer;
* **12,110** pool clues score at or above that, i.e. the canonical tuple's descending rank in the
  pool is ~12,110 - it is not marginal, it is deep;
* the 50th clue scores **0.8612803490** (`it's justice too bed aimed`), so the gap to the cutoff is
  **0.0405108161**;
* the best pool clue containing `hid` scores **0.8739047068** (`hid josh dashed oop add aim`), i.e.
  `hid` itself is not the problem - the other four words are.

If that holds, then **no amount of extra enumeration or a better budget allocation can put the
canonical tuple in the top 50**, because the final scorer rates it 0.0405 below the cutoff. Under
that reading the remaining acceptance gap is a **scoring-margin** problem, and it is the one surface
no live or closed front owns.
