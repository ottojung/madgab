# Two approximate-mode acceptance tests fail at the milestone base

## Goal

`cargo test --release` is green on the accumulation branch, or the red tests
state a behaviour decision that is recorded somewhere durable.

## Context

Found while landing the behaviour-preserving speedups in
[w-7fa26c](w-7fa26c.md). At `d46d154` (the content of
`post-milestone-acceptance`) these two tests in
`tests/corpus_integration.rs` fail, and they fail identically on an unmodified
checkout of that commit:

- `approximate_finds_classic_madgab_resegmentation` — expects
  `hits justice dupe hid came` in the top 50 for `It's just a stupid game`;
  the top proposals are now dominated by `it justice ...` spellings.
- `approximate_finds_recognize_speech_resegmentation` — expects
  `wreck a nice beach` in the top 50 for `recognize speech`; the top proposals
  are now `yet a guys each`, `yeah can i.'s it's`, ... and
  `wreck a nice pitch` at rank 8 of the printed head.

Both words are still *reachable* in the fuzzy lattice
(`acceptance_sequences_are_reachable_in_fuzzy_lattice` passes), and both
resegmentations still appear in the proposal list at a much lower rank. The
milestone commit that changed the scoring and the diversity selection
(rhythm axis, boundary-keyed diversity, larger completed pool) moved them out
of the top 50 without any of these tests being updated.

This blocks the "`cargo test --release` is green" completion criterion in
[w-7fa26c](w-7fa26c.md), and it cannot be fixed by a behaviour-preserving
change: the two options are to change the search (which is a clue-quality
decision) or to change what the tests assert (which is a product decision about
how strongly approximate mode must surface the canonical Mad Gab wording).

## Completion criteria

- A decision is recorded: either the search is changed so both canonical
  resegmentations are back in the top 50 (a clue-quality change, probably
  [w-4b1e07](w-4b1e07.md) territory), or the tests are rewritten to assert
  something that is actually true today — e.g. that the canonical wording is
  present in the full proposal set, with an explicit note that rank is not
  guaranteed.
- `cargo test --release` is green.
- If the tests are relaxed, the reason and the accepted rank/position are
  written down here, not just in the test body.

## Handoff / notes

Not started. Do not fix this as part of a pure performance change: it will
show up as an unrelated failure and tempt a behaviour edit. Reproduce with
`cargo test --release --test corpus_integration` on any checkout of `d46d154`
or later.
